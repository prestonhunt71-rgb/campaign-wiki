# Revision 3.0.20 — Configurable Relationship Display Depth

- Add separate world settings for the relationship display depth of Metaplots, Arcs, Places, People, and Images.
- Offer direct-only, two-step, and three-step display options.
- Preserve current behavior by default: Metaplots display two steps; all other top-level trees display direct relationships only.
- Apply the selected depth consistently to Article relationship cards and the **Explain Relationships** report.
- Keep relationship depth independent of visibility, parentage, and sidebar placement.
