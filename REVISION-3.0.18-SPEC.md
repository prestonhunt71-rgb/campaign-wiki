- Align Article quotes with the title at the left side of the Article header.
- Restrict the Arcs sidebar mirror to genuine Arc Articles, preventing related People Articles from appearing as top-level Arcs.
- Give Images, People, and Places ancestry precedence when classifying multi-parent Articles.
- Keep MacGuffins and other Images Articles in their proper sidebar tree even when they also have Arc or Metaplot relationships.
