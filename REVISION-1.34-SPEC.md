# Campaign Wiki 1.34.0 (staged, not bundled)

- Adds a per-parent sidebar category organization mode:
  - Alphabetical (default)
  - Article Count (largest first, alphabetical ties)
  - Manual
- Automatic modes preserve any previously saved manual order. Returning to Manual restores it.
- Existing saved category orders remain Manual for backward compatibility.
- Standardizes article section and subsection heading typography, rules, and vertical spacing.
- Places linked thumbnail categories inside the responsive Connections grid alongside ordinary relationship groups.
- Ensures manual content and GM Notes precede Connections, followed finally by Featured as actor in, Scene appearances, and Taxonomy in that order.
- Bottom-aligns the three-step specific-article relationship controls and reserves equal label height to correct wrapped-label misalignment.
