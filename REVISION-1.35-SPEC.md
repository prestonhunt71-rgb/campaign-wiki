# Campaign Wiki 1.35.0 (staged, not bundled)

- Every ordinary Connections group now renders linked artwork tiles instead of a text-only list.
- Articles without artwork receive a neutral linked placeholder tile.
- `Link To` and `Linked From` relationships are grouped by target section, such as `Linked Handouts`, `Linked Actors`, or `Linked Vehicles`.
- A Connections group with more than four tiles spans the full responsive grid width; smaller groups remain column-sized.
- Generated collapsible Featured-as-actor and Scene-appearance lists remain unchanged at the end of the article.
