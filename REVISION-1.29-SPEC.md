# Campaign Wiki 1.29

This release builds on the 1.28 taxonomy redesign and QA fixes.

- Selecting a relationship category rebuilds the individual target
  autocomplete from the articles inside that category and its descendants.
- Team members are again individually selectable for the Golden Agents and all
  other hero or villain teams.
- Previous / Next controls navigate the exact sidebar folder used to open an
  article and loop continuously.
- Folder navigation works in reading and editing views. Save keeps the editor
  active for the current article; Previous or Next opens the adjacent article
  in editing mode. Unsaved changes are protected by confirmation.
- Known Associations contains Teams and Actors / By Employer assignments only.
- All explicit taxonomy assignments remain available in the collapsed Taxonomy
  section.
- Featured As Actor In remains a collapsed, compact list.
