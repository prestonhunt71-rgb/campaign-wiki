# Revision 3.0.25 — Article Identity and Relationship Polish

- Prefer the longest matching Article title or alias when automatic links overlap.
- Treat ordinary and pasted non-breaking whitespace equivalently when matching multi-word Article links.
- Keep automatic linking limited to the first eligible mention and preserve player visibility restrictions.
- Correct direct People and Images category-role classification without changing stored Article relationships.
- Place organizer and category relationships at the bottom of an Article's relationship sections.
- Derive an Affiliation's First Appearance from the earliest visible dated Session involving one of its directly affiliated members.
- Prevent new Articles and title changes from duplicating another Article title anywhere in the Wiki, regardless of category, capitalization, or equivalent spacing.
- Grandfather existing duplicate titles so older Articles remain editable and migrations preserve existing content.

Existing databases, relationships, visibility, linking-category selections, sorting, relationship depths, custom wording, artwork paths, and page settings are preserved.
